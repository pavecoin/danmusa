Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2024

This Privacy Policy governs the Danmusa Songs mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "App"). The App provides an official music streaming platform for the musical works of artist Danmusa New Prince.

Summary: The App does not require user accounts, does not show advertisements, does not track your personal identity, and stores all playlist/favorite preferences strictly on your local device.

1. Information We Collect & How We Use It

We believe in privacy by design. The App operates without gathering personal identifiable information (PII):

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any email address or personal credentials to use the App.
  • Local Storage (Preferences): Your liked songs, custom playlists, and last played song are saved locally on your Android device using encrypted DataStore Preferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • Network Connections: The App connects to public internet endpoints solely to fetch song catalog metadata (JSON) and stream public MP3 audio files. No device profile, unique ID, or personal data is transmitted.

2. Advertising & Monitization (No Ads / No AdMob)

The App is 100% advertisement-free:

  • We do NOT integrate Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) or any third-party ad networks.
  • We do NOT collect, request, or use Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) or device finger-printing.
  • No user profiling or targeted advertising is conducted.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Protecting children's privacy is extremely important to us:

  • The App is suitable for general audiences and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.
  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can immediately delete any such information.

4. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area Users)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access & Erasure: Since all user preferences (liked songs and playlists) are stored locally on your device, you hold complete control over your data. You can erase all stored data at any time by clearing the App's data in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.
  • Data Transfers: No personal data is stored or transferred across borders. Network requests for media streaming are strictly stateless fetching of audio files.

5. CCPA & CPRA Compliance (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • We do NOT sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.
  • We do not track consumers across third-party websites or services.

6. Permissions Requested

The App requests standard Android permissions strictly required for core audio playback functionality:

  • INTERNET: Required to download song metadata and stream online audio tracks.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display music playback controls in your notification bar and lock screen.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required to maintain music playback while the App is running in the background.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Required by ExoPlayer to prevent audio playback from stuttering when the screen turns off.

7. Third-Party Media Providers

Song metadata and audio streams are hosted on secure remote content delivery networks (CDNs) or public GitHub endpoints. These servers log standard web server access logs (such as IP address and user-agent string) solely for network security and bandwidth management.

8. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your local data. All network calls utilize Secure HTTP (HTTPS) protocol to ensure audio streams and song metadata are transmitted safely.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
